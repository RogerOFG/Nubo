# Sounds

The sounds of the original app are reserved by its author and are not part of
this repository. Put your own **.wav** files here, named like the sounds the app
plays, and the build ships them:

peek, open, close, hover, blip, slap, annoyed, dizzy, greet, work, finish,
error, approval, question, approve, gulp, tick, send, love, pop, proud, wink,
yawn, attach, think, search, rate, sleep, greeting

(e.g. `finish.wav`). A missing file just means that event is silent. You can
also drop files into the sounds folder that Settings → General opens, without
rebuilding.
